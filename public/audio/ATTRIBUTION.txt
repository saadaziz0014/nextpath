The author describes a Pacific National NR Class locomotive idle recording,
tagged 7FDL / V16. This is an engine-family substitute, not a Pakistan AGE-30
recording. The app loops the supplied loop, changes its playback rate and volume
according to throttle/load and speed, and blends low-level synthetic turbo and
rail sounds. Full throttle is an approximation from an idle recording, not an
authentic recorded notch 8. The bundled file has not otherwise been edited.

AGE-30 engine specification: https://pakistanrail.com/locotech/pages/AGE30.htm
Exact AGE-30 reference video (not downloaded or redistributed):
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0kzVrRpbD8

License attribution is also displayed in the train controls. The horn remains
an original synthesized horn. If loading or decoding fails, the engine uses its
procedural fallback. No account or third-party audio request is needed at runtime.
